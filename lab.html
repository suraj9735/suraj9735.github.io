Play with vibrations & control

Each simulator solves the equation of motion numerically in your browser (4th-order Runge–Kutta). Move a slider and the response updates instantly.

Free vibration Forced vibration PID control Animations

1 · Free vibration

m·ẍ + c·ẋ + k·x = 0. Release the mass from x₀ and watch it decay.

2 · Forced vibration & resonance

m·ẍ + c·ẋ + k·x = F₀·sin(ωt), starting from rest. Bring the frequency ratio r = ω/ωₙ close to 1 to see resonance.

3 · PID position control

A controller pushes the mass to a target position: F = Kp·e + Ki·∫e dt + Kd·ė, with e = target − x. Tune the gains to trade speed against overshoot.
